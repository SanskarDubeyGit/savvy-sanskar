There are things that become clearer when you stop trying to make them clear.

## The first idea

This sample article exists to demonstrate the long-form reading experience — and how an image with a caption sits inside an article's body.

![A quiet, uncluttered desk](./building-things-slowly-workspace.jpg)
*This is a placeholder — swap it for your own photo whenever you're ready.*

Writing can be simple, spacious, and worth taking your time with. The image above is placed with plain Markdown:

```md
![A quiet, uncluttered desk](./workspace.jpg)
*This is a placeholder — swap it for your own photo whenever you're ready.*
```

The italic line directly under an image automatically becomes its caption — no extra markup needed.
